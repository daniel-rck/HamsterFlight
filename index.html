HamsterFlight

Launch the hamsters - as far as they will fly.

      In flight, the map at the top right shows where they are - dots in these colours.

          Click or Space to jump, click again to hit the pillow, then hold to glide. One swing per jump; a miss costs no turn. P, Esc or the corner button pauses; a click resumes. M or the note mutes the music, S the sound effects; F goes full screen and I opens this help. A gamepad works too: A is the button, Start pauses.
